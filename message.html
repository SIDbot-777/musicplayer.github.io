A Poem for You

Ki mujhe maut ka shauk tha, tune kine ki aas jagayi
ki tujhe dekhne ke baad meri ankhon ne mujhse hi sharmayi.

Ki hasta raha mann mera, aisi tune baat batayi,
bhula is duniya ko, aise teri yaad aayi.

Pucha jab tune "kya hua?" to sari baat nikal aayi,
mai dukhi hua nahi hu, jabse tere se aankh milayi.

Teri zaroorat thi mujhe, isliye lagta hai
tu meri zindagi mein aayi.
                             -Jivesh Dahiya
